Miku's UwU Breeder

Plan your Pal breeding routes with a stronger Hatsune Miku-inspired look. Choose from every Pal in the loaded 1.0 breeding roster and every named passive skill in the current game list. The app builds a shortest species-breeding chain and shows the Paldeck number at every step.

Use the visual Pal dropdowns — each choice shows the Pal picture, name, and Paldeck number. At each breeding step, use Path Options to choose a route using Pals you actually own. If you switch away from a partner, that Pal is excluded globally from every later dropdown. Use Clear exclusions to bring those Pals back. At every step, keep breeding until the child inherits the selected passive, then use that child as the carrier in the next step.
If automatic loading fails, the app will retry through two browser-safe data routes. You can also load the JSON files manually.
